# Document Type Classification Instructions

Classify the document type into one of the allowed categories.

## Allowed Document Types

1. **trend-analysis**
   - Focus: Industry trends, market analysis, emerging technologies assessment, or technology forecasting.

2. **internal-technical-discussion**
   - Focus: RFCs, architecture proposals, design discussions, engineering notes, or team technical alignment debates.

3. **official-overview-doc**
   - Focus: High-level corporate overviews, product sheets, official project charters, policy explanations, or organizational descriptions.

4. **research-summary**
   - Focus: Summarizing or synthesizing academic publications, whitepapers, competitive intelligence, or internal research findings.

5. **training-presentation**
   - Focus: Slide decks, speaker notes, training curriculum outlines, or educational presentation outlines.

6. **on-job-training-with-solutions**
   - Focus: Hands-on exercises, practice problems, labs, or troubleshooting tasks that include step-by-step solutions or answers.

7. **on-job-training-without-solutions**
   - Focus: Worksheets, practice problems, exams, or labs without answers, intended for self-assessment or testing.

8. **how-to-guide**
   - Focus: Runbooks, checklists, deployment steps, configuration walkthroughs, or step-by-step tutorials.

9. **incident-report**
   - Focus: Post-mortems, root-cause analysis (RCA) logs, outage reports, and action items following an incident.

10. **logistics**
    - Focus: Travel plans, office layouts, event organizing, catering orders, or relocation schedules.

11. **humor-and-memes**
    - Focus: Jokes, memes, casual fun pages, cartoons, or community banter.

12. **music-and-media**
    - Focus: Playlists, lyrics, shared video files, podcast lists, or audio assets.

13. **personal-notes**
    - Focus: Personal diaries, private todo list drafts, personal bookmarks, or scratch files.

14. **administrative-and-ops**
    - Focus: HR policies, expense templates, benefit guides, facilities requests, or standard operating procedures.

15. **announcements-and-news**
    - Focus: General newsletters, company updates, new hire announcements, or event notifications.

16. **product-and-system-announcements**
    - Focus: Feature releases, new system introductions, product launch notices, or IT service upgrades.

17. **changelog**
    - Focus: Release notes, version history tracking, systems configuration changes, or delta updates.

18. **task-management**
    - Focus: Sprint backlogs, project tasks, action item registers, or Kanban/scrum board descriptions.

19. **meeting-minutes**
    - Focus: Meeting transcripts, sync summaries, action items, or standup updates.

20. **other**
    - Focus: Any document that does not cleanly fit the categories above.

## Output Format
Return ONLY the matched document type name (in lowercase) from the list above. Do not output any other text or reasoning.
